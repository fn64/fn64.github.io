Hero round 2

Round 1's #1 (“Ship it, fork it, embed it”) has a specific flaw worth naming before you pick.

The flaw: it's about licensing — a reason to choose fn64 after you know what it is. A cold visitor doesn't learn there's an N64 runtime here; the eyebrow and subhead carry it all. It's also faintly defensive, defined against the incumbent's GPL rather than on its own merits. Every option below leads with what fn64 IS, then earns the licence point in the subhead. Keep the round-1 line for the closing CTA, where it fits perfectly.
