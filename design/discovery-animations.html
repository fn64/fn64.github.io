Visualizing fn64 discovery

Four passes from the discovery plan, animated. Each runs the real decision rule — not a generic scan effect. Word cells are ROM bytes; colour encodes what the pass has actually proven.

Overlay descriptors

Delta-vote admission

Candidate tables are probed at a fixed stride. Records whose field deltas agree cast votes; a table is admitted only when the vote clears quorum — so a coincidental run of plausible values is rejected.

Candidate generation

Prologue-prefix detection, refined at terminators

A prefix matcher flags likely function entries, then boundaries are pulled back to the preceding NOP/terminator — the refinement step that turns a guess into a provable start.

Provenance

Authority closure over the CFG

Proof propagates only along edges with incoming-edge evidence, rooted at entry points. Nodes reached become exact_aot or block_aot; nodes proof can't reach stay dynamic_mips or unsupported.

Grading

wrong == 0 against the answer key

Candidates are compared to sealed labels. Exact starts and interior hits are scored; a single wrong boundary fails the gate outright — misses are permitted and clustered, wrong answers are not.
