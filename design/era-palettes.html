Era palettes

Built from what the research actually confirmed, not vibes. Each palette shows a hero block, a button pair, a data table, and an Edge-style rating line.

Two confirmed findings driving these: (1) Edge printed its typefaces in every colophon — ITC Franklin Gothic Heavy / Gill Sans Bold / Bell Gothic — plus a literal “Fifth colour: Pantone 8303c” spot plate beyond CMYK. Not the Eurostile/Bank Gothic I'd guessed. (2) Edge had no score box. The rating was one line at the foot of the last column, spelled as words — “Eight out of ten” — never a big numeral. That's the model for the recall figures: refuse to monumentalise the number.

1 · Edge fifth-colour (Pantone 8303c)

The confirmed anomaly: a metallic teal-grey spot ink printed alongside CMYK. Restrained, editorial, and almost nobody revives it — so it reads as specific rather than generic-retro. Paper is warm uncoated stock.
